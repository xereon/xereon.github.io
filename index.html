About

I've been writing code since the PHP days and never stopped tinkering. Lately most of my time goes into browser-native apps — things that lean on WebGL, the Web Audio API and modern streaming, with no server to babysit and nothing to install.

I'm also a heavy follower of the open-source AI scene; a good chunk of my GitHub is forks of agent frameworks and local model tooling I've been reading through and running.

Everything here is open source. Fork it, break it, tell me what you'd do differently — say hi on GitHub.
